Pitt & Carnegie Mellon

Two research universities share one neighborhood. Pitt is a state-related university chartered in 1787. Carnegie Mellon is a private university Andrew Carnegie founded in 1900. Their campuses meet at Schenley Park, with the Carnegie Museums and Library between them.

01 · Location

Neighbors across Schenley Park

Oakland is Pittsburgh's university district, about 2.5 miles east of Downtown along Forbes and Fifth Avenues. Pitt fills the west side around the Cathedral of Learning. CMU sits to the east on the edge of Schenley Park. The Carnegie Institute complex, opened in 1895, stands between them.
